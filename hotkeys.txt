# BetterSpellWheel - Spell Hotkeys
#
# Bind a utility spell to a key and cast it with one press, no wheel needed.
# Edit this file, then restart the game. Lines starting with # are ignored.
#
# How to bind in game: give a slot a key below, open the spell wheel (Q),
# point at a spell and press that slot's key. The slot takes that spell.
# Point at the centre (no spell) and press the key to clear the slot.
# Close the wheel and press the key to cast.
#
# Slot spells: the spell's in-game name, e.g. Rocksplosion, Windstep, Harvest. None = empty.
# Slot keys: UE4SS key names, e.g. Z, T, F5, NUM_ONE, XBUTTON_ONE (mouse 4). none = unbound.
# Unbound keys in Dragonwilds: I L N O T U Z. Avoid F10 (UE4SS console) and F11 (fullscreen).
#
# Read-only: casting a hotkey does exactly what picking the spell from the wheel does.
# The game checks unlocks, runes and cooldowns itself. Your save is never touched.

# Quick pick (CS buy-menu style). With the spell wheel OPEN, the number row picks a skill then a spell:
#   first number  = pick a skill (1..12 = keys 1 2 3 4 5 6 7 8 9 0 - =, clockwise from the top of the ring)
#   second number = cast that spell in the opened skill's fan (same as clicking it)
